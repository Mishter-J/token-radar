How the implied value is worked out

implied = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity. Growth is the protocol’s seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50% … +150%, weighted 0.8. Maturity is 0.50 + (days listed, capped at 1,460) / 1,460 × 0.50, so a protocol listed four years ago counts at full weight and a new one at half. A protocol with no listing date is counted at 365 days. Every row on this page can be re-derived from those three numbers — the detail view shows each one.

The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: the 40 protocols at or above $50m of locked value with no token recorded, plus the 160 highest-implied-value pre-token protocols the watchlist leaves out, below that floor and above it. Protocols whose token already trades are kept apart below, at the market’s own market cap — they are never ranked against the ones still to come.

A protocol “records no token” when its DefiLlama record carries none; that is not proof it has none. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token is issued.
